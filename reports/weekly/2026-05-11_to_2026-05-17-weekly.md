_QEC / Fault Tolerance • arXiv RSS quant-ph • 2026-05-12_

**Why it matters:** QEC and logical-qubit work is a key indicator for scalable, fault-tolerant quantum computing.

**Key points:**
- The hypergraph product creates a quantum stabilizer code from two input classical linear codes; a paradigmatic example being the surface code as a hypergraph product of two classical repetition codes
