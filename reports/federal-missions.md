### [Genesis Mission](https://www.energy.gov/undersecretaryforscience/genesis-mission/genesis-mission)

**ACTIVE · CRITICAL** · national mission · Lead: **Department of Energy, White House Office of Science and Technology Policy**

Build an integrated American Science and Security Platform connecting federal scientific datasets, supercomputers, AI systems, experimental facilities, and emerging quantum systems to double the productivity and impact of American research and engineering within a decade.

- Announced: **2025-11-24**
- Phase: **execution**
- Domains: Artificial intelligence, Quantum information science, High-performance computing, Energy, Discovery science, National security

### [QC-ADDS](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/ushering-in-the-next-frontier-of-quantum-innovation/)

**ACTIVE · CRITICAL** · national effort · Lead: **White House Office of Science and Technology Policy, Department of Energy**

Pursue a quantum computer at a scale intended to initiate quantum-enabled scientific discovery, with the intent to deliver at least one system to a DOE facility and make it available to the scientific community to the extent possible.

- Announced: **2026-06-22**
- Phase: **specifications and partnership planning**
- Domains: Quantum computing, Scientific applications, Performance assessment, National security, Post-quantum cryptography
- Next milestone: **2026-09-20 — Publish a summary of QC-ADDS technical specifications**

### [Quantum Genesis](https://www.energy.gov/science/articles/energy-department-announces-initiative-create-and-deploy-worlds-first)

**ACTIVE · CRITICAL** · federal initiative · Lead: **Department of Energy Office of Science** · Parent: **Genesis Mission**

Develop and deploy a scientifically relevant, fault-tolerant quantum computing capability for research, supported by a DOE Q Competition, a national user facility, and focused quantum-application research.

- Announced: **2026-06-23**
- Phase: **competition and capability development**
- Domains: Fault-tolerant quantum computing, Logical qubits, Quantum applications, Quantum supercomputing, AI and HPC integration
- Next milestone: **2028 — Demonstrate fault-tolerant systems with logical qubits in the low hundreds**

Recent official updates:
- 2026-06-23 — [DOE establishes the Quantum Genesis initiative](https://www.energy.gov/science/articles/energy-department-announces-initiative-create-and-deploy-worlds-first)
