READING THE DASHBOARD

What the labels mean

Rules are deterministic and configurable

↗

Rising

Evidence in the latest seven days increased by at least 50% versus the preceding seven days.

→

Stable

Recent evidence changed, but not enough to meet the rising or declining threshold.

↘

Declining

Recent evidence decreased by at least one-third versus the preceding seven days.

●

Critical importance

The theme contains evidence scoring 100 or higher.

●

High importance

The theme contains evidence scoring 50–99. Medium is below 50.

◎

Actionable

A high- or critical-importance theme is rising and merits near-term review.

◉

Watching

The signal remains relevant but does not meet the actionable or stale rule.

◌

Stale

No supporting evidence has appeared for more than 14 days.

✓

Confidence

High means at least five items from three sources; medium means at least two; low means one.

!

Source health

Healthy has no warning days. Degraded retains at least 80% reliability; failing falls below 80%.

R

Readiness stage

The highest stage supported by public PQC evidence. It is not an audit of internal systems.

H

Historical evidence

Official-source evidence imported outside the daily window; it cannot create retroactive alerts.
