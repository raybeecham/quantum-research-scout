READING THE DASHBOARD

What the labels mean

Rules are deterministic and configurable

↗

Rising

Evidence in the latest seven-day period increased by at least 50% versus the preceding seven days.

→

Stable

Recent evidence changed, but not enough to meet the rising or declining threshold.

↘

Declining

Recent evidence decreased by at least one-third compared with the preceding seven days.

●

Critical importance

The theme contains an evidence item scoring 100 or higher, indicating exceptional strategic relevance.

●

High importance

The theme contains an evidence item scoring 50–99. Medium importance is below 50.

◎

Actionable

A high- or critical-importance theme is rising, making it a candidate for near-term review or follow-up.

◉

Watching

The signal remains relevant, but it does not currently meet the actionable or stale rule.

◌

Stale

No supporting evidence has appeared for more than 14 days.

✓

Confidence

High means at least five evidence items from three sources; medium means at least two; low means one.

!

Source health

Healthy has no warning days. Degraded retains at least 80% reliability; failing falls below 80%. Expected arXiv weekend idle days are excluded.

N

New alert

The condition was not active during the prior alert evaluation. Persistent conditions remain active without repeatedly appearing as new.

A

Active watch item

An entity or technology appeared in supporting evidence within the last seven days.

Q

Quiet / dormant

Quiet means the last match was 8–30 days ago; dormant means more than 30 days without matched evidence.

✓

First-party covered

At least one enabled official feed, sitemap, newsroom, or blog is configured for the organization.

!

Coverage gap

Gap means no official source or evidence exists. Third-party means evidence exists, but no enabled first-party source is configured.

V

Verified source

The scheduled collector has successfully reached this source since observation tracking began.

F

Fresh source

The source has a dated item within the configured freshness window. Stale means its latest dated item is older; unknown means collected items have no usable publication date.
