## Method

Probabilities use bounded, published heuristics from mission priority, recent official activity, funding, awards, open opportunities, and milestone timing. Forecasts retain their probability history and are scored with the Brier rule after resolution. If supporting linkage is corrected, the forecast is withdrawn transparently and is not included in calibration.
