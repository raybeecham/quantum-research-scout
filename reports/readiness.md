## Methodology

- **Awareness:** Public evidence acknowledges quantum cryptographic risk, standards, or migration needs.
- **Inventory:** Evidence addresses discovery or inventory of cryptographic assets and dependencies.
- **Planning:** Evidence describes readiness assessment, governance, roadmaps, transition planning, or crypto-agility.
- **Pilot / Testing:** Evidence documents testing, a pilot, prototype, proof of concept, benchmark, or interoperability exercise.
- **Production:** Evidence explicitly documents deployed, production, or completed PQC implementation.

The highest explicitly matched stage is shown. Backfilled evidence contributes to the scorecard but is marked historical and cannot generate retroactive alerts.
