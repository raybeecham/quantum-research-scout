**Key points:**

### Tightly Secure and Modular Post-Quantum TLS Handshakes
_Crypto Agility • IACR ePrint • Published 2026-11-11 01:25 America/Chicago • CRITICAL 90_

**Why it matters:** AI security work matters for model abuse, prompt-level compromise, alignment risk, and adversarial use of agentic systems.

**Key points:**
- In the post-quantum (PQ) migration of the TLS~1.3 handshake, the classical Diffie--Hellman key exchange is replaced with a post-quantum key encapsulation mechanism (KEM)
- Furthermore, KEMTLS builds upon PQ TLS~1.3 by replacing signature-based authentication with KEM-based authentication
- However, all existing analyses of PQ TLS~1.3 and KEMTLS incur at least quadratic reduction losses in the number of sessions, and achieving a tightly secure construction remains an open problem
