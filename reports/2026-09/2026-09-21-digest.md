### Too Small to Hide: Single-Trace Key Recovery from ML-KEM Key Generation
_PQC • IACR ePrint • Published 2026-09-21 04:57 America/Chicago • CRITICAL 112_

**Why it matters:** PQC migration signals affect algorithm adoption, certificate and protocol readiness, and exposure to harvest-now-decrypt-later risk.

**Key points:**
- Key generation in ML-KEM (CRYSTALS-Kyber) samples a short secret from a centered binomial distribution (CBD) and immediately transforms it with the number-theoretic transform (NTT)
- The CBD sampler stores each coefficient as a signed $16$-bit two
- Each execution draws fresh randomness, so an attacker obtains a single trace and cannot average
- We show that one power trace of the optimized pqm4 implementation on an Arm Cortex-M4 suffices, and that the two operations leak complementary information

[Open item](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2137)
